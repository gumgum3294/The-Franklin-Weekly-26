Frequently asked questions

What exactly do I get for $30 a month?

A locally written email newsletter about Franklin, Michigan, delivered every Saturday at 3:00 p.m. It covers village and civic news, school and community updates, local events, road and safety notes, business news, and neighbor stories — all in one readable issue.

What's the difference between monthly and yearly?

Nothing about the newsletter itself — both plans get the same Saturday issues. The monthly plan is $30 billed each month, and the yearly plan is $371 billed once for the full year. The yearly plan simply means one payment and no monthly reminders.

When does the newsletter arrive?

Every Saturday at 3:00 p.m. If you subscribe mid-week, your first issue arrives the following Saturday.

Is this written by a real person in Franklin?

Yes. The Franklin Weekly is written locally and focuses only on Franklin and the immediate area. There's no syndicated national content and no auto-generated filler.

Payments are processed through Whop, a secure third-party checkout platform. Your card details are handled by Whop and are never stored on this website.

Yes — you can cancel your subscription at any time through your Whop account. You'll keep access through the end of the period you've already paid for.

Absolutely. Local tips, event listings, business openings, and community announcements are what make the newsletter work. Email Anthonyasad2015@gmail.com and we'll take a look.
